Key Research Takeaways
  • FT-Transformer Champion: Outperforms all 10 baselines with R² = 0.9896 and RMSE = 126.21 Wh/m² via Feature Tokenizer embeddings and multi-head self-attention.
  • Non-Linear Interactions: Self-attention effectively captures complex multi-variable interactions (e.g. DNI modulation during winter zenith angles).
  • Spatial Generalization: Evaluated under 43-Fold LOSO CV with R² = 0.9784 across all Saudi weather monitoring stations.
  • Automated MLOps: Optuna Bayesian hyperparameter search coupled with MLflow model registry promotion gates.

Dataset Overview & Metadata
  • Dataset: King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM) Solar Network
  • Geographic Scope: 43 Stations across all provinces of Saudi Arabia
  • Temporal Window: 2017 – 2021 (Monthly aggregated observations)
  • Total Records: 1,649 clean observation rows
  • Input Dimensionality: 21 continuous meteorological & irradiance features
  • Target Variable: Global Horizontal Irradiance (GHI) in Wh/m²
